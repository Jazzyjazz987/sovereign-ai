24 proposals · 22 survived · 716475 tokens · 13 agents

## Highest-leverage change

Create a dedicated Anthropic Console workspace for the stack and mint a workspace-scoped API key with a hard, server-side monthly budget limit - then make that the only Anthropic credential the stack holds and strip ANTHROPIC_API_KEY from the litellm service in docker-compose.yml. This is roughly 30-60 minutes of console work and one compose edit. It removes the worst quantifiable blast radius in the system: today a leaked ANTHROPIC_API_KEY burns unbounded real money and bypasses the entire stack, because T5_MAX_CALLS in main.py is a per-process counter that resets on every container restart and the durable t5_ledger only sees requests that actually pass through main.py. A provider-side hard cap is the only control that survives a process restart, a wiped ledger table, and the on-prem box being gone or compromised; revocation becomes one click from any browser even if the box is unreachable; and it yields per-workspace usage attribution for the CNIL and finance trail at zero code cost. There is no RGPD or sovereignty downside because the bytes sent to Anthropic are unchanged. The only precondition is verifying that the Anthropic account tier enforces hard-block workspace spend limits rather than soft email alerts - if only alerts are available, this becomes a strong detection aid and containment leans on the key removal plus the rotation runbook plus the T5-fail-closed-rate Prometheus alert.

## Top proposals (ranked, post-critique)

### 1. Safeguarding lane: deterministic lexicon short-circuit before routing, plus a safety system prompt on the local tiers
_facet: liability-human-escalation_

**Why:** This is the one item in the whole review that is non-negotiable and also nearly free. An uncensored-adjacent 7B answering 'mon chef me harcele, que faire' is simultaneously a safeguarding failure, an Article 9 special-category incident on an identifiable agent, and institutional liability - and cascade_router.py today routes purely on complexity keywords with no such gate, while query_ollama sends prompts RAW with no system prompt. Refuse-and-route-to-named-humans is the only defensible architecture, it costs almost no compute, nothing leaves the box, and it does not touch the frozen cascade. The paired fix - adding a safety system prompt to every local-tier call via the already-adopted prompt pack - is a tiny change that closes the residual-risk case where oblique phrasing slips the lexicon and still reaches a local model.

**Sketch:** New config/safeguarding_lexicon.yaml (deliberately generous match list for harcelement / VSS / souffrance au travail / discrimination / idees suicidaires / danger immediat, reviewed periodically with medecine du travail) and config/safeguarding_card.yaml (Polynesia-localised: Direction de la sante, SOS Suicide Polynesie, referent harcelement, service social, DRH-QVT, every number verified from the fenua; versioned with a named owner + re-validation date, shipped through the content channel). In api/main.py, run the lexicon screen as the very first step of the request path, before kNN classification and any tier call; on match return the card and do NOT call T1-T5, do NOT read or write cache, do NOT create a corbeille ticket containing the text - increment only a time-bucketed anonymous per-category counter so an increment cannot be correlated with an access-log line. Separately, add a safety system prompt to query_ollama via config/prompts.yaml. Audit the real log surface (uvicorn/proxy, oauth2-proxy, LiteLLM Postgres persistence) and publish a precise 'what is retained' statement instead of an absolute 'not stored' claim. Work: ~1 week eng; card authoring plus written sign-off from QVT/medecine du travail that they will be the named landing point.

### 2. Cited-fiche gate + accountability registry as code (merge liability P1 and P4)
_facet: liability-human-escalation_

**Why:** The strongest structural fix in the set. It attacks the actual liability - the real T4/legal tier is a phantom (llama2:7b / dolphin-mixtral historically) and complexity-score routing can send an RGPD question to a 7B - by making the legal/admin tier a citation-or-redirect gate that structurally cannot emit an unsourced legal claim at any tier including T5. CPU/GPU cost is negative (retrieval over a small corpus beats generation), it shrinks the Article 46 transfer surface because these clusters stop going to T5 entirely, and it lands accountability on an institution that can be accountable (DPO/DGRH/DAF) rather than a community fine-tune. P1 (runtime) and P4 (governance) are the same system seen from two sides and must be built as one. The honest failure mode - a high redirect rate early on exactly the questions agents most want help with - is acceptable only if the corpus is seeded by real query volume and the tool is framed as an orientation aid, otherwise it drives users to shadow ChatGPT.

**Sketch:** New config/legal/content_owners.yaml: one row per answerable cluster (service responsable, valideur nomme, date de validation, peremption staggered 6-12 months, textes de reference). In api/main.py, for kNN labels statutaire / RGPD-conseil / paie-prestations / droit-des-usagers the router may return only verbatim-quoted + linked fiche text (no model summarisation); below retrieval confidence or with no owning fiche it returns a hard 'consultez [service competent]' card with zero model prose. The classifier LABEL is the hard gate for T5 and all local tiers on these clusters - not the complexity score. Add a legal-content re-check on redaction/generic clusters to close the 'redige un mail expliquant les regles de conges' reframing hole. CI: only a YAML schema error breaks the build; a missing/expired owner fails SAFE to the runtime redirect card plus a warning, with a T-60-day upcoming-expiry alert to the editorial owner. Unanswerable high-stakes questions become an async reponse-differee ticket to a named functional queue (DPO for RGPD, DGRH for statutaire, DAF for paie), exposed per-domain only once that direction commits to staffing it. The safeguarding screen (item 1) takes strict precedence - those categories never become a ticket. Work: L - small engineering (YAML + CI check + router branch + reuse of the corbeille), dominant cost is organisational (build the fiche corpus by descending query volume, pull in DGRH/DAF/DPO, push the editorial role toward ~0.5 FTE).

### 3. Second-island CPU-only DR box + quarterly game-day, with a 3-class state split and continuous append-only journal shipping (merge backup P3 and P1)
_facet: backup-dr-single-box_

**Why:** Today 'box down for a day' equals a total outage for 5500 agents across every island, with no backup/restore procedure at all. This turns that into graceful degradation: canonical answers + T1 local inference + read-only corbeille history stay up, new T5/legal is refused with the existing typed degradation contract. CPU-only DR is not even a speed regression because ~3 tok/s is today's production baseline. The quarterly game-day is the only thing in the entire review that actually proves a restore works - without it the RPO/RTO numbers are fiction. The 3-class split collapses the DR problem: the only legally and financially irreplaceable data (the Article 46 transfer journal and the real-money t5_ledger) is ~1 MB/day of append-only rows that a satellite link can ship continuously; everything else has genuinely weak requirements by design.

**Sketch:** Name a decommissioned CPU-only box at an existing second-island site as standing backup destination + restore target; same git repo, scripted reduced-.env sync (NO ANTHROPIC_API_KEY, NO LITELLM_MASTER_KEY - degraded mode needs neither). Add a mandatory recovery class to api/legal/data_manifest.yaml: (1) legal/money core - api/main.py writes each transfer-journal and t5_ledger entry as an append-only line to a local JSONL file at commit time in addition to the Postgres row; a systemd timer rsync/scp's it off-box every 1-5 min (no logical replication, no replication slots). (2) corbeille - nightly encrypted pg_dump, accepted RPO 24h. (3) reconstructible (LangGraph checkpoints, LiteLLM metadata, pgvector, T0 cache) - documented acceptable-to-lose. 'Mode degrade DR' = manual DNS / reverse-proxy flip by named ops (record TTL 60-300s, pre-provisioned access), serving only the canonical answer base (flat file, no pgvector rebuild) + T1 + read-only corbeille history. Quarterly game-day: ops performs the real flip and serves real traffic ~1h; assertions = measured RTO under 1h and restored transfer journal matches primary within RPO; failback + corbeille reconciliation step written down. Add the DR nightly dump and the off-box journal file to the erasure-reaper target list and the monthly drill scope. LUKS full-disk encryption on both boxes as the at-rest baseline (covers the Postgres volume). Work: M - hardware exists; ~1 week to script the sync, the JSONL shipper, and the flip runbook; ~half a day per quarter ongoing.

### 4. Secrets containment: workspace-scoped Anthropic key with a server-side hard cap, plus a unified rotation/compromise runbook with a departure trigger (merge secret P1 and P4)
_facet: secret-key-management_

**Why:** The single .env on a host that publishes :8888/:4000/:11434/:5432 with no auth is the worst exposure in the system, and the two cheapest controls close most of it. A leaked ANTHROPIC_API_KEY today burns unbounded real money and bypasses the entire stack - T5_MAX_CALLS is a per-process counter that resets on every container restart, and the durable t5_ledger only sees requests that go through main.py. A provider-side hard monthly cap is the only control that survives a restart, a wiped table, and the box being gone, and revocation is one click from anywhere. The runbook closes a total gap - there is no rotation story at all - and unifying rotation with compromise-response keeps it exercised quarterly instead of written once and rotted. The departure trigger is the sharp insight for a 2-3 person team: one leaver is a third of everyone who ever held the secrets.

**Sketch:** Create a dedicated 'sovereign-ai-prod' Anthropic Console workspace; mint a workspace-scoped key with a hard server-side monthly budget limit; make it the only Anthropic credential the stack holds. Remove ANTHROPIC_API_KEY from the litellm service in docker-compose.yml (main.py calls the anthropic client directly; the LiteLLM Anthropic route is unused). Keep the durable t5_ledger + config/budget_envelopes.yaml as the sovereign record and local early-warning. Add a Prometheus alert on the T5 fail-closed rate - a leaked-but-undetected key burns the full envelope every month and the only visible symptom is 'T5 silently degrades to local'. New docs/secrets_runbook.md: per-secret one-paragraph rotation recipe + cadence (quarterly 'secrets day' for LITELLM_MASTER_KEY / VLLM_API_KEY / POSTGRES_PASSWORD / Grafana; annual for the future Entra secret; provider-schedule for Anthropic; compromise-only for any journal key). Hard rule: any team departure triggers full rotation of provider-revocable + box-local secrets within 48h plus a written note. Fold in the Postgres footgun (POSTGRES_PASSWORD env var is init-only on an existing volume - the recipe must spell out ALTER ROLE + update every DSN + restart) and a drain / low-traffic window before restarts given ~3-minute CPU-bound answers with no client copy. Migrate postgres/grafana/langgraph to Docker file-secrets now; SOPS+age on the secrets file; defer the litellm _FILE shim. Work: S-M - console setup ~1h, runbook ~1 day plus a rehearsal, file-secrets migration ~1 day. CAVEAT: verify the Anthropic plan tier enforces hard-block spend limits, not just email alerts (operator question below).

### 5. erasure_class backbone: R/P/E on every store, one erase_subject() primitive, DPO-signed class assignments, and a DPO-channel dsr_scan operator tool (merge DSR P1 and P3)
_facet: data-subject-rights-ops_

**Why:** RGPD/CNIL is a hard constraint and a data-subject request currently has no answer across ~11 heterogeneous stores. This converts the problem into 'erasure across the few subject-keyed stores plus a documented Article 11 basis for the rest', which a 2-3 person team can actually operate and prove, and it extends the already-adopted data_manifest.yaml. Not building a citizen IAM + DSR ticketing subsystem is the right call for this team on M365 F3 and satellite links - the government DPO already owns statutory intake. The load-bearing weakness is honest and must be handled: class P ('irreversibly pseudonymised, out of scope') is a claim, not a fact - GLiNER misses Polynesian surnames, matricule agent, numero CPS/DSFE and free-text third-party singling-out - so every class assignment needs a written re-identifiability argument the DPO signs, not a developer label.

**Sketch:** Extend api/legal/data_manifest.yaml with a mandatory erasure_class per store: R (resolvable/subject-keyed), P (irreversibly pseudonymised - requires a documented EDPB singling-out/linkability/inference assessment signed by the DPO), E (ephemeral, TTL in minutes, e.g. in-memory pii_mapping). Verify and pin LiteLLM to metadata-only (no prompt/response persistence) or classify its store R. Response cache + embeddings default to R unless key and stored content are proven free of citizen/third-party identifiers; only the canonical-answer base is P. One primitive erase_subject(subject_key) in api/ iterating class-R stores only, deleting matching rows, writing a tombstone = salted hash of subject_key + timestamp + stores touched (no clear-text key). CI linter: fail if any store lacks a class or a class-P store grows a column matching a re-identification pattern; document the linter's no-semantic-detection limit in the DPIA as a known residual risk. One operator tool dsr_scan(identifiers) under the named-access policy: normalised/fuzzy name search over raw-query class-R stores with mandatory human confirmation of candidate hits; output is a scoped signed attestation (names the stores checked; states derived analytics stores are identifier-free by design per DPIA section X) filed in the DPO case file. Agent access/erasure of their OWN corbeille tickets is self-service in the authenticated PWA; the DPO channel handles citizen DSRs and agent requests touching third-party data. Keep the scan as a named purpose in the Article 30 register. Work: M - ~1 week eng plus DPO review sessions. Ship after items 1 and 2 land (proof-of-absence is load-bearing on the PII layers).

### 6. Adoption front door: login-time onboarding, three answer labels, the structured fiche de demande, and a signaler-to-canonical loop with shadow-egress as the north-star (merge adoption P2, P3, P1, P4)
_facet: adoption-onboarding-trust_

**Why:** The actual purpose of this stack is to stop agents pasting citizen data into ChatGPT; a program that is technically excellent but colder than ChatGPT fails at exactly that. These four proposals form one coherent front door. Onboarding-as-a-property-of-login is the only fundable answer to 'who trains 5500 agents' for a 3-person team and covers every future hire. The three labels give calibrated (not maximal) trust - agents who learn 'reference validee DSI' vs 'genere localement - non verifie' vs 'reponse cloud' weight answers correctly. The structured fiche teaches async + non-conversational + fallible before the agent types a word and matches the ~3 tok/s serialized single Ollama lane. Making shadow-egress the headline KPI is the single most valuable framing in the review: it aims the program at its real purpose and protects it from being killed on vanity 'low adoption' numbers while it is in fact working.

**Sketch:** Build all of this as amendments to the round-3 PWA corbeille and prompt pack, not new workstreams. Onboarding (P2): on first authenticated session serve a ~4-minute walkthrough as 100% static assets (CI assertion: zero calls to the shared Ollama lane) - submit a throwaway question, see a canned degraded answer with the real banner, see a canned sourced answer, click signaler once, then read + acknowledge the versioned charte. Split the gate: charte acknowledgement is hard-blocking, the tutorial allows a bounded 'reprendre plus tard'. Stagger charte re-acknowledgement on version bumps. Report completion as a coverage metric, not a trust metric. Fiche de demande (P1): subject + body + a non-authoritative intent picker (kNN taxonomy frozen at 8-12 items owned by the editorial role - routing hint only, the server independently decides tier eligibility) + a 'pas de dossier usager' checkbox treated as onboarding-time expectation-setting, never an enforcement control (the PII pre-screen stays the real barrier); 303 to the corbeille ticket; non-conversational; 'question de suivi' with a hard context-carry byte cap. Scope the intent field under the round-3 identity-linked-data policy with CSE/CST information. Labels (P3): three only - 'Reponse de reference validee DSI' / 'Reponse locale generee - non verifiee' / 'Reponse cloud (anonymisation automatique)'. Ship the labels now (no dependency). The honest-decline path is gated on the editorial owner being confirmed + the canonical base having real coverage + the classifier existing; until then a conservative static 'je ne suis pas sur' threshold with decline-rate monitored as a health metric. Signaler loop (P4): editorial triage with two terminal outcomes (fingerprinted dated canonical entry, or closed with a one-line enum reason) + one reporter notification; PII scrubbed on ingest; hard SLA ('traite sous 10 jours ouvres ou ferme') + spot-audit of 'closed - non reproductible'; withhold the all-users 'N corrections' counter until it is genuinely healthy. North-star = a small panel of separate lines (async-answer usefulness, 30-day repeat-use, canonical-hit-rate trend, and inverted, aggregate egress to chat.openai.com/claude.ai/gemini from DNS/proxy logs - strictly aggregate, zero per-user, declared to CSE/CST as employee-activity monitoring with a registre entry, one trend line among several). Gate go-live on canonical-base day-one coverage. Work: L across the bundle but mostly amendments; the binding dependency is the ~0.3-0.5 FTE editorial owner and CSE/CST lead time.

## Contradictions (and resolutions)

- System of record for tickets: backup-DR P2 wants the requesting agent's M365 mailbox/OneDrive to BE the system of record for completed reponse-differee tickets, which explicitly diverges from the round-3 adopted 'PWA identity-owned server-side ticket store as system of record', and also overlaps backup-DR P3's 'second-island box serves read-only corbeille history'. RESOLUTION: the corbeille stays the system of record (round 3, settled). M365 is a durable REPLICA only, written to ONE DSI-owned SharePoint/mailbox resource - never per-agent mailboxes (a tenant-wide Graph application write permission on a box that publishes ports with no auth is an unacceptable blast radius). It is co-equal with the second-island nightly dump, not a cache; reconcile on recovery. It cannot be the durable store anyway because M365/Entra/satellite outage is already an accepted degradation mode - it is unavailable exactly when a regional event kills the box. The re-identified answer body landing in M365 needs its own DPIA section, TTL and reaper coverage, or store only the anonymised answer there.
- Does the Article 46 transfer journal need a keyed per-payload digest at all? Secret P3 (basket C) assumes yes and builds a DPO-custodied, two-site-sealed, annually-re-verified age-identity escrow around an HMAC key; backup-DR P1 then has to continuously ship that key's signing half off-box; the secrets runbook has to carry a 'compromise-only' rotation path for it. RESOLUTION: default the transfer journal to METADATA ONLY (timestamp, tier, token count, GLiNER version, label set, outbound-scan result, closed-enum purpose - zero query-derived text or digest). Then basket C disappears, no HMAC key enters the future-secrets list, the DR legal core has no signing key to ship, and the runbook is simpler. Round-3's own DSR-ops analysis says the honest answer to almost every citizen DSR is 'nothing retained', so data-subject matching against historical journal entries is very unlikely to be a real requirement. Only if the operator confirms that requirement: give the key basket-B treatment in Anone's own container secret + Postgres authentication + LUKS on that volume + one sealed offline copy in the DSI safe (not DPO-custodied, not two sites - cold start must never block on a person).
- Three overlapping crypto-at-rest designs: backup-DR P4 (envelope encryption with per-domain/per-window DEKs), secret P2 (SOPS+age + Docker file-secrets, explicitly no Vault), and the LUKS recommendation that both assessments add. RESOLUTION: LUKS full-disk encryption on both primary and DR box is the baseline - uniform coverage including the Postgres volume (which holds the sensitive rows SOPS-on-.env never touches), survives unattended reboot, fewest moving parts. SOPS+age on the secrets file ADDITIONALLY, migrating postgres/grafana/langgraph to file-secrets now and deferring the litellm shim. For genuinely off-site backup blobs only, a small FIXED set of 4 per-retention-domain keys (age/gpg), rotated yearly not per-window, escrowed 2-of-3 Shamir or two sealed printed copies at two sites - never bus-factor-1. Drop per-time-window keys (a monthly key does not enable per-subject erasure, it only lets you drop a whole month, which plain blob deletion already does).
- Release P1 pre-prod (a second full Docker Compose stack under COMPOSE_PROJECT_NAME=sovereign-preprod on the same CPU box) versus the RAM and core budget of that box: a second Ollama loading Mistral 7B + Llama 7B concurrently with prod is ~+11 GB plus a second GLiNER plus second Postgres/LiteLLM/LangGraph, while the 200-item eval and residual prod traffic contend for the same cores at ~3 tok/s, roughly halving prod throughput and silently backing up the async lane. RESOLUTION: pre-prod runs ONLY the app-tier containers (LangGraph, Anone, LiteLLM) on isolated ports plus a scratch Postgres schema, pointed at the PROD Ollama model-server read-only. An Ollama or model-weights change is validated by the round-3 convoi plus its own eval run, never by standing up a second model daemon. Verify RAM headroom before first use; bound the deploy window (~60 min off-peak) and plan the queue drain.
- Adoption P1's non-conversational fiche model (a follow-up is a new linked fiche) versus genuinely iterative tasks (debugging a PowerShell snippet), and adoption P3's honest-decline path whose trigger condition 'would only reach T1/T2' is satisfied by essentially every query on the CPU-only box today (T3/T4 off, T5 capped/gated) - so at launch with a thin canonical base the system would decline the majority of day-one queries, which is honest but indistinguishable from broken and pushes users to shadow ChatGPT. RESOLUTION: adopt P1 as an S-sized amendment to the round-3 PWA with a 'question de suivi' that carries prior fiche + answer forward under a hard byte cap. Sequence P3's decline path AFTER the canonical base has real coverage and the kNN classifier exists; until then run a deliberately conservative static 'je ne suis pas sur' threshold and monitor decline-rate as a health metric so it never silently becomes the default response. Gate the whole adoption front door on canonical-base day-one coverage.

## Facets / concerns still missed after 4 rounds

- Security incident detection and RGPD breach notification. The box has published :8888/:4000/:11434/:5432 with no auth and the review never asks: how is a compromise detected (there is no SOC, the ops team does not watch Grafana), what is the containment runbook, and how does the DSI meet the CNIL 72-hour personal-data-breach notification obligation? The secrets runbook covers key rotation on compromise but not detection or notification.
- Program-level resourcing as a single number. Every facet quietly adds recurring human load - editorial/triage owner pushed toward 0.5 FTE, three functional escalation queues (DPO/DGRH/DAF), quarterly DR game-days, secrets days, safeguarding-card and lexicon maintenance, canonical-base curation - and nobody has totalled the FTE and euro ask across the whole adopted set. This is the top risk to the entire review: most items degrade to 'high redirect rate' or 'broken promise' if the roles are not staffed.
- Foundational lawful basis for the processing itself. The DPIA-as-code register documents stores, but the review never establishes the authorizing act (arrete / deliberation, mission d'interet public under Art. 6(1)(e)) that legitimises processing 5500 agents' queries, nor the formal information des agents and CSE/CST consultation as a prerequisite gate before the authenticated PWA can launch at all.
- Joiner/mover/leaver lifecycle for 5500 agents. When an agent leaves the government and their Entra account is deactivated, what happens to their corbeille tickets, their onboarding/charte record, and their derived data? And the secret P4 'departure triggers rotation' rule needs a reliable HR/Entra feed to the 2-3 person ops team - neither the data cleanup nor the trigger plumbing is designed.
- Language coverage beyond French. 'French-first' is stated but agents and citizens use reo Maohi (Tahitian), Marquesan, Paumotu. The review flags Polynesian surnames as a PII-detection weakness but never asks how the models, GLiNER, the kNN classifier, the canonical base and the safeguarding lexicon behave when a query - or pasted citizen text - is in a Polynesian language.
- Access-log and observability data governance. uvicorn/proxy access logs, oauth2-proxy logs, Prometheus/Grafana series - the safeguarding item flags 'audit the log surface' locally, but there is no cross-cutting retention period, access policy, or check for whether these logs contain query fragments (error strings, URLs) that turn a supposedly metadata-only surface into identity-linked personal data.

## Open questions for the operator

- Is the ~0.3-0.5 FTE editorial/triage owner a funded, named, standing role with a backup? Almost every adopted item (canonical base, signaler loop, fiche taxonomy, content_owners.yaml re-validation, safeguarding card upkeep) collapses to a broken promise without it. If it cannot be funded at 0.5 FTE, which items are cut?
- Will the DPO, DGRH and DAF each formally commit to staffing a functional escalation queue with a response SLA? If not, those domains get the redirect card with no callback promise, and the human-escalation path the review assumes does not exist.
- Does the Anthropic account plan tier enforce HARD-BLOCK workspace spend limits, or only soft email alerts? This determines whether the highest-leverage change is a real financial backstop or just a detection aid.
- Is there a specific decommissioned CPU box plus rack space at a named second island, and can the 2-3 person ops team absorb ~half a day per quarter for DR game-days plus ~half a day per quarter for secrets days?
- Has CSE/CST consultation and formal information des agents been initiated, and with what lead time? The authenticated PWA, the charte gate, the intent field, the identity-linked data policy and the shadow-egress KPI all depend on it and cannot launch before it completes.
- What is the monthly T5 (cloud) budget envelope figure in euros, who is the single named holder authorised to lift the hard cap, and does leadership accept that a too-low figure means T5 fails closed to local for the rest of the month?
- Does the DSI have a genuine, documented need to match a data subject against historical Article 46 transfer-journal entries, or is audit/proportionality the only purpose? This decides whether the journal is metadata-only (simplest) or needs a keyed digest plus Postgres auth, LUKS and offline key custody.
- Launch strategy: big-bang to all 5500 agents, or phased by direction? This drives the onboarding gate design, day-one capacity on the single serialized Ollama lane, and how much canonical-base coverage is required before go-live.
- Will DRH and the secretariat general co-sign the charte d'usage as a formal note de service (giving it disciplinary weight), rather than it being a standalone oauth2-proxy click-through with weak standing in French public-sector employment law?
- Is the interim CPU worker-VM's hosting itself sovereignty-compliant for government personal data? A rented hyperscaler VM running inference on agent queries is a sovereignty problem independent of its cost, and the DR and capacity designs assume it stays in play.
